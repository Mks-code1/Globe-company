#### Our Products
Three kinds: **Hot-Rolled, Pre-Engineered & Open-Frame Truss Buildings.** Fast to manufacture and assemble. Balances budget, aesthetics and performance. Satisfies engineering, acoustic, thermal and seismic requirements.

- **Hangars** (image: `services/hangars.jpg`) — Erected much faster than traditional construction. Simple and cost-efficient to expand. Useful for storage. Design is easy to modify, allowing extensions, movable walls, and connection of new buildings to the original hangar.
- **Trusses** — Provide wide column-free spaces inside buildings, as in stadiums, malls and wedding halls.
- **EOT Cranes Girders & Gantry** (image: `services/EOT.jpg`)
- **Installation Work** — Skilled technicians carry out the work according to the timeline, at the site.
